Welcome

Welcome to The Somnolescent Dragon.

You'll find ten galleries, each built around its own collections of carefully curated ultrawide wallpapers. Some galleries are already deep, others are just beginning, and all of them will continue to grow over time.

Our current focus is seasonal, so expect new Halloween collections first, followed by Christmas and other holidays as the year turns. That said, the Dragon does not curate strictly by calendar. If the right idea appears, if a particular mood takes hold, or if a creative suggestion sparks something interesting, a new collection may arrive at any time.

Check back here for notes on upcoming ideas, collections in development, new gallery additions, and other signs that the Dragon has been dreaming again.
