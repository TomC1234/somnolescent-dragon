New

Well, here we are. The Somnolescent Dragon is open, the gallery doors are unlocked, and we're starting with over 200 wallpapers across ten collections. Everything you see is new because none of it existed here yesterday.

Flights of Fantasy is the flagship for now — 64 wallpapers of warm cheerful fantasy that I'm genuinely proud of. Castles and Keeps and Wondrous Water are close behind with their own deep libraries. Cosmic Codex and Dreamscapes are smaller but curated with real care.

Halloween and Haunts is actively building — the Pumpkin Family in particular is shaping up to be something special. Musical Spice has 40 prompts written and is next in the generation queue. Winter's Embrace, Christmas Heart, and Magical Miscellany are coming as the seasons turn.

Updates bi-weekly. The dragon is always dreaming. Welcome.
